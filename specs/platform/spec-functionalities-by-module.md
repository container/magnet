# MAG-NETic Platform - Functionalities by Module

Purpose: provide a high-level, non-prioritized catalogue of platform functionalities for later prioritization and delivery planning.

Status: platform reference

This catalogue groups confirmed platform functionality into larger, prioritizable areas. It does not replace the detailed requirements in the related platform specifications.

---
